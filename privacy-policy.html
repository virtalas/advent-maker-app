Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and do not collect, store, or share any personal information directly from users of this app.

Use of TelemetryDeck to analyze app usage

We use the privacy-friendly analytics service TelemetryDeck (provider: TelemetryDeck GmbH, Von-der-Tann-Str. 54, 86159 Augsburg, Germany) to analyze usage data. The use is based on Art. 6 para. 1 lit. b GDPR, as we require reliable and efficient tools for collecting app usage data in order to fulfill the contract with you, our customer.

What data is transferred?

The data processed by TelemetryDeck is completely anonymized and does not allow any conclusions to be drawn about personal information.

The following data is collected, among other things:

  • an anonymized, untraceable user ID (per app installation),
  • actions defined by the app publisher (e.g., "app launched," "settings opened"),
  • a rounded timestamp (to the nearest hour),
  • device metadata (e.g., system version, app version, device type),
  • additional metadata defined by the app publisher (e.g., "number of items in the database").

What is not stored?

  • No IP addresses (not in logs, not in the database),
  • No cookies or tracking technologies,
  • no persistent identifiers that could be traced back to individuals.

The source code of the TelemetryDeck SDK is completely open source and available on GitHub: https://github.com/TelemetryDeck

Further information on the exact data processing by TelemetryDeck can be found at: https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy and at https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/guides/privacy-faq/

WishKit Feedback Framework

This app uses WishKit, a third-party feedback and feature-request service provided by WishKit UG. When you submit feedback, feature requests, or interact with the WishKit interface within the app, the following information may be collected and processed by WishKit:

  • The feedback or feature request content you submit
  • Optional information you choose to provide (such as email or name), if requested by the app
  • Technical information such as IP address, device details, and usage data automatically collected by WishKit's servers

This information is transmitted directly to WishKit and handled according to WishKit’s own privacy policy. We do not control or have access to WishKit’s internal data processing.

For more information, please review WishKit’s privacy policy:
https://www.wishkit.io/privacy-policy

YouTube Content

This app may display embedded videos from YouTube, a service provided by Google LLC. When you view or interact with these videos, YouTube may collect information such as your IP address, device information, and viewing activity. This information is collected and processed directly by Google in accordance with its own privacy policy.

For more information, please visit Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not have access to or control over any data collected by YouTube.
