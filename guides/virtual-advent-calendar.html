Virtual Advent Calendar: Make One Online & Send It Anywhere

Updated for the 2026 holiday season · 4 min read

A virtual advent calendar is the December classic without the cardboard: 24 doors on a screen, one unlocking each day, each hiding something you chose — a photo, a video, a message. You make it in minutes and it can reach anyone, anywhere, today.

There are two ways to make one, and the difference matters more than most guides admit.

Advent Maker — the virtual advent calendar app Free · no sign-ups for anyone · your photos never touch a server

Link-based websites vs. a calendar file

Website makers (the ones that give you a shareable link) upload your photos and videos to their servers; your recipient views the calendar in a browser. It works, but your family photos now live on someone's server, subject to their retention policy, account requirements, and this-year's-business-model.

Advent Maker takes the file approach: the whole calendar — doors, photos, videos, messages — is packed into a single file on your device. You send that file like you'd send anything else: AirDrop, WhatsApp, Telegram, email. The recipient opens it in the free app, and the calendar lives on their device.

  • Private: nothing is uploaded anywhere; no company holds your Christmas
  • No accounts: neither of you signs up for anything, ever
  • No expiry: a link can die in January; a file on their phone is theirs to keep
  • Native experience: doors, countdown, and the snowman run as a real app, not a webpage

Making yours in four steps

  1. Install the free Advent Maker app on iPhone, iPad, or Mac.
  2. Create a New calendar and fill the 24 doors — the step-by-step guide covers this in detail.
  3. Tap Share calendar and choose how to send the file.
  4. Your recipient installs the same free app and opens the file. Done — doors unlock daily from December 1st.

Who virtual calendars are perfect for

  • Family abroad — no shipping, no customs, arrives in seconds
  • Long-distance couples — the dedicated guide has 24 door ideas
  • Grandparents — daily grandkid content, zero password resets
  • Friend groups — send the same calendar to everyone and compare doors in the chat
Devices: Advent Maker runs on iPhone, iPad, and Mac (iOS 18.5+). Every Apple device in the family can make, send, and receive calendars.
