A Christmas Countdown App with a Door for Every Day

Updated for the 2026 holiday season · 3 min read

Most Christmas countdown apps show you a number: 43 days, 12 hours, 6 minutes. Useful for about four seconds a day. An advent calendar turns the same countdown into a ritual — every morning in December there's a door to open, and behind it something a real person put there for you.

Advent Maker is a countdown you can give to someone: it counts the days to Christmas and rewards each one.

Advent Maker — the countdown with 24 surprises Free · no ads · iPhone, iPad & Mac

How the countdown works

Every calendar in Advent Maker has 24 numbered doors — December 1st through Christmas Eve. Doors unlock strictly one per day; tap a locked one and the app tells you how many days remain, answering the eternal "how many sleeps until Christmas?" without any math at the breakfast table.

Because you fill the doors yourself — photos, messages, videos, a playable snowman — the countdown gets more exciting as the number gets smaller. The December 24th door is the finale you design.

Countdown ideas that beat a timer

  • For kids: the daily door is the countdown — pair each day with a tiny mission or joke (see the kids guide)
  • For a partner far away: count down to Christmas and to your reunion at once (long-distance guide)
  • For yourself: make a calendar of 24 small December treats — one good coffee, one walk, one film per door — and open your own door every morning
  • For the family group: everyone gets the same calendar file and compares doors in the group chat each morning
No peeking, guaranteed: unlike a paper calendar with bendable flaps, the app physically can't open tomorrow's door today. The suspense survives even the most determined seven-year-old.

Free, private, and off before New Year

Advent Maker is free with no ads, no subscription, and no account. It collects no data — worth checking on any countdown app you hand to a child. And when Christmas has come and gone, the calendar remains as a keepsake of the year's photos and messages.
