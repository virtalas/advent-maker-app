Photo Advent Calendar: Turn 24 Memories into a Countdown

Updated for the 2026 holiday season · 4 min read

A photo advent calendar hides one of your own pictures behind each of 24 doors. Instead of chocolate, the person you love opens a memory every morning in December: the beach in July, the dog in the leaves, the blurry karaoke night. By Christmas Eve, they've relived your whole year together.

Printed photo calendars are lovely but cost money and take weeks to arrive. The digital version is free, takes about fifteen minutes, and can be delivered to the other side of the world today. Here's how to do it well.

Advent Maker — free photo advent calendar app Your photos behind 24 doors · captions & video too · no login

How to pick your 24 photos

The hardest part isn't the app — it's choosing. A structure helps:

  • Chronological year: January to December, two doors per month. The calendar becomes a year-in-review that ends at this Christmas.
  • Themes: trips, meals, friends, pets, "faces you make", inside jokes — one theme per week of Advent.
  • Then and now: alternate old photos with recent ones. Devastatingly effective for parents and grandparents.
  • People: for a family calendar, give each family member their own run of doors.
Tip: Order matters. Put strong photos on December 1st (first impression), the 12th (mid-month slump), and the 24th (finale). The Christmas Eve door should be the photo — or better, a video — you'd frame.

Building it in Advent Maker

  1. Install the free Advent Maker app on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.
  2. Create a New calendar and name it — "Our Year 2026" works every time.
  3. Tap each door and choose Edit photo to add the picture for that day.
  4. Add a caption with Edit text — one line about why that moment mattered turns a nice photo into a small gift.
  5. Sprinkle in a video door or an interactive snowman so not every day is the same.

Sharing your photo calendar

Tap Share calendar and send the calendar file via AirDrop, WhatsApp, Telegram, or email. The recipient opens it in the free app — no account, no upload to any photo service. Your pictures travel directly from your device to theirs, which matters when the content is 24 personal family photos. From December 1st, one photo unlocks per day.

Who to make one for

  • Your partner — especially across a distance
  • Parents and grandparents — see the grandparents guide
  • Your kids — a year of them growing up, one day at a time
  • A best friend — 24 shared disasters and triumphs
