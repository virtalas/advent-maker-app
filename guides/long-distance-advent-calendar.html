Long-Distance Advent Calendar: 24 Daily Surprises for Your Partner

Updated for the 2026 holiday season · 4 min read

December is the hardest month to be apart. Everyone else is doing cozy things together, and you're doing time zones. A long-distance advent calendar fixes a small but real piece of that: every single morning until Christmas, your partner opens a door you made for them — a photo, a video of your voice, a message that proves you were thinking of them weeks ago.

Shipping a physical calendar abroad is expensive and slow (and customs eats chocolate). A digital one arrives in seconds, and you can make it tonight.

Advent Maker — send an advent calendar anywhere Free · no accounts for either of you · works on iPhone, iPad & Mac

Why an advent calendar beats a daily text

You already text every day. The difference is premeditation: a calendar is 24 surprises you prepared in advance, sealed so neither of you can rush ahead. The app unlocks exactly one door per day and shows the countdown to Christmas — which, if you're flying to see each other, is also the countdown to your reunion.

24 door ideas for couples

  • Photos: the first photo of you two · the place you met · their side of the bed · what you see on your walk to work
  • Videos: a 20-second "good morning" · you cooking their favorite meal badly · a tour of your Christmas decorations · you singing one line of "your song" (see the full ideas list)
  • Messages: "the moment I knew" · a compliment they don't hear enough · plans for your first day together after the distance ends
  • YouTube links: the song from your first date, a trailer for a film you'll watch together on a call
  • The snowman: Advent Maker's interactive snowman makes a perfect low-stakes palate cleanser between emotional doors
December 24th: make it count. A longer video message, or — if you're seeing each other over the holidays — a photo of your flight confirmation.

How to send it across any distance

  1. Get the free Advent Maker app and create a New calendar.
  2. Fill the 24 doors (do it over a few evenings — it's genuinely fun).
  3. Tap Share calendar and send the file via WhatsApp, Telegram, or email — whatever you two already use.
  4. They install the free app, open your file, and find the calendar on their "Received calendars" shelf.

No login, no server, no subscription — the calendar goes from your phone to theirs. Doors unlock by date on their device, so even with a 7-hour time difference, each morning has its surprise.
